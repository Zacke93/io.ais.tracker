Requires an AISHub membership with your own NMEA station (≥10 vessels/7 days, ≥90% uptime). Max 1 request/minute — use one username on ONE device only. aishub.net

Shadow mode polls AISHub and logs a freshness/coverage comparison (SHADOW_COMPARE) without affecting bridge status. Switch to "Both" after reviewing at least 48 h of shadow data.

Choose debug level for troubleshooting. Higher levels provide more detailed logs but may impact performance.

📋 Connection status

After saving your API key:

  • ✅ Valid key: the app connects within a few seconds
  • ❌ Invalid key: you get a timeline notification after repeated failed attempts
  • 🔧 Troubleshooting: check your key at aisstream.io
  • 📡 AISHub: the first poll starts within ~1 minute; look for AISHUB_POLL / SHADOW_COMPARE lines in the app log

The connection status is shown on the "Bridge Status" device in your home.
